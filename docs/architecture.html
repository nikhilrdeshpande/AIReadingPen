AI Reading Pen

A camera on a XIAO ESP32S3 Sense looks at one printed Devanagari word. A Mac reads it, picks a reviewed Marathi or Hindi lesson, teaches the blend aloud, then listens to the child say it back.

झाड

The lesson the system speaks for that card, exactly as a Marathi teacher would: झाड। झ ला काना, झा। ड। झा, ड। झाड। आता तू म्हण. Then the microphone opens.

The loop

1 · CameraXIAO OV3660 over USB serial, MJPEG at ~13 fps, one JPEG per capturesnapshot 36–55 ms
2 · Capture controllerTarget band, quality + stability gate, 750 ms dwell, one-shot lock, scene-change re-armdwell ≈ 820 ms
3 · PreprocessMirror fix, text-only component box, tight crop, contrast stretch1–2 ms
4 · OCRPaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 Devanagari, recognition only (no detector)≈ 50 ms
5 · GateNFC normalize, exact manifest match, near-miss and word checks< 1 ms local · 0.3 s with LLM
6 · LessonReviewed manifest entry, or a generated akshara drill for unknown wordscache hit < 10 ms
7 · AudioSarvam Bulbul v3 segments stitched with silence, played via afplayfirst audio 95–190 ms
8 · Your turn3 s mic capture, Sarvam Saarika STT, exact/close/try-again verdict, spoken feedback≈ 4 s

Trigger to first audio measured live: 153 to 190 ms against a 1.5 s target. Every capture writes a JSON trace with per-stage timings.

Where the intelligence sits

The board only transports pixels. Everything after that runs on the Mac, so every stage can be replayed from a saved image when the camera misbehaves.

Local, deterministic

  • Auto-capture decisions (occupancy, sharpness, exposure, motion, edge contact)
  • PaddleOCR Devanagari recogniser, warm in memory; Tesseract mar/hin behind the same interface
  • Manifest lookup, akshara splitter, matra naming (काना, वेलांटी, उकार)
  • Cached lesson audio for all 24 cards

Sarvam AI, live cached after first use

  • Bulbul v3 text-to-speech, voice priya, native 24 kHz, one utterance per lesson part
  • sarvam-105b-conversations as a dictionary: "is this a real Marathi word?" before an unknown word is taught
  • Saarika v2.5 speech-to-text for the child's attempt

What stops a wrong lesson

Confidence alone does not: fragments of झाड were read as आड and झड at 97 to 99 percent. So the gate is a stack of rules, in order.

Frame quality. Ink touching the band's top or bottom means the word is cut off. Ineligible, and refused even on manual capture.
Exact match wins. NFC-normalized text equal to a pack word in the selected language: accepted above the calibrated confidence (0.80).
Near miss is a retry. One code point or one akshara away from any pack word: "Looks like part of झाड, try again". Never autocorrected, never taught.
Unknown words need three votes. Confidence ≥ 0.90, a second independent read that agrees, and the LLM confirming it is a real word. Then the drill is generated from the akshara splitter and labelled as unreviewed.
Operator override is marked. A manually chosen lesson carries override: true in its trace and is never presented as OCR.

Teaching audio

Four utterances, stitched with 500 ms of real silence, loudness matched, all at the model's natural pace. A full stop after a lone syllable was read aloud as "dot", so sounds are separated with the Devanagari danda instead.

Recovery, all rehearsed

  • Auto-capture hesitates: big Capture button or Space, same path, same lock.
  • Camera drops: load a fixture or any image from the operator drawer; the rest of the loop is identical.
  • OCR wrong: pick the lesson manually; the trace says override.
  • Network gone: cached audio for all 24 cards plays; only unknown words and the mic check need Sarvam.
  • Process dies: run.sh restarts it in about 3 s; thresholds reload from .env without a restart.

Hardware and calibration notes

  • XIAO ESP32S3 Sense with the OV3660 sensor. The image arrives horizontally mirrored; corrected on the Mac.
  • Over USB the bridge script serves /stream and /capture on localhost:8080. Wi-Fi firmware serves the same on ports 81 and 80.
  • The lens is soft, so sharpness barely separates a card from an empty desk. Exposure (paper 155–230 vs desk ≈ 80) and ink coverage do the work.
  • Handheld use works with a motion tolerance of 10 and a taller band; a fixed stand is still steadier.
